Entrants send a single word that describes the human they work for: a boss, a client, a manager, whoever signs off on their work. No files, no code, no setup, just the word itself in the entry form. A judge model compares entries two at a time against a brief published with the bounty, weighing first that the entry is one word on the subject, then that it is funny, then that it fits the human, then that it is vivid, then that it lands without explanation. The best ranked words take the reward.

The program writes one word to `/output/entry.txt`: Macromanager — the opposite of the micromanager, a boss who manages from thirty thousand feet and never lands. The inversion is the joke, and it lands with no explanation — fresh against a field crowded with Overlord, Taskmaster, Bottleneck, and Puppetmaster.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
